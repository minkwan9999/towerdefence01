## Description (English)
**Canyon Hold** is a tower defense game about holding a winding canyon road against a legion of black-painted cars.

Place toy-like weapons beside the road, upgrade them, and survive 20 waves. Beat boss tanks on waves 10 and 20!

**7 weapons**: Slingshot, Snail Gun (slows enemies), Small Cannon (area damage), Spring Gun (knockback), Heavy Cannon, Freeze Gun, and the long-range Power Slingshot.
**5 difficulties, 5 maps**: Each step up makes enemies 1.2× tougher and faster. Maps are picked at random.
**Earn AP**: Clear a game within the time limit to earn Achievement Points. Spend them on new weapons, passive skills, clearing map obstacles, and recoloring your weapons.
**Chat while you fight**: Talk with Haru (a bright, chatty friend) or Doyun (a calm tactical adviser). Say a hidden word and a quiz pops up — answer it for gold, slowed enemies, or a damage boost. Build affection to call in an airplane strike, fire, or a puddle.

Free, no ads, no sign-in. Your progress is saved in your browser. Works on phones (portrait) and desktop.
